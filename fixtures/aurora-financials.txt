MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS

Revenue grew 15% year-over-year, driven primarily by expansion into three
new metropolitan markets and a 9% increase in average membership pricing.
However, gross margin declined from 55.0% to 47.8% despite the revenue
growth. Management attributes this primarily to higher equipment
procurement costs following a supplier contract renegotiation in Q2
FY2025, and to elevated energy costs at newly opened locations that have
not yet reached target utilization. Operating expenses grew more slowly
than revenue (8% vs. 15%), reflecting cost discipline in corporate
overhead, but this was not enough to offset the gross margin compression,
resulting in a decline in both operating income and net income
year-over-year despite higher revenue.

No new equity or debt financing was issued during FY2025. The company's
new markets are expected to reach target membership utilization by
mid-FY2026 according to internal projections, though these projections
have not yet been independently validated.
