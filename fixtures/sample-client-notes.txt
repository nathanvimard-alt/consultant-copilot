Client: NorthPeak Outdoor Gear (fictional demo company)
Internal notes from account team, Q3 review

Retention update: Logo retention dropped from 92% to 84% over the last two quarters.
The decline is concentrated almost entirely in customers acquired through the paid
social media channel in the last 6 months — retention for customers from organic
search and referral channels has stayed flat at 93%.

Support team flagged a 40% increase in tickets mentioning "shipping delays" starting
in June, coinciding with the switch to a new fulfillment warehouse partner.

No pricing changes have been made in the last 12 months.

Customer success has not yet run an exit survey for churned accounts this quarter,
so we do not have direct reasons from customers who left.
